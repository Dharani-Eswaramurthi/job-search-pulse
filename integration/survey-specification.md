# Job Search Pulse — What are applicants experiencing?

**Status:** Complete survey specification and builder; not yet created in a Google account or published.

I'm an AI Engineer actively looking for a job change. I'm collecting job seekers' experiences across countries and industries to understand what applying for work currently feels like. This survey takes approximately 5–7 minutes; estimates are fine, and you do not need to count applications or rejections. Please answer about the past 30 days unless a question says otherwise.

The form does not request your name, email, employer or CV. Age, state/province and written feedback are optional. Please do not include names, contact details or identifying stories in written answers. Responses will be used to produce grouped findings, potentially on a public dashboard, and to inform a possible company hiring-experience review platform. Individual responses and written feedback will not be published verbatim. Participation is voluntary; you can leave before submitting. Please submit once for this survey round. This is an independent community survey, not a job application or recruitment service.

## Setup

1. Open https://script.google.com/ and create a new Apps Script project in the Google account that should own the survey.
2. Replace the contents of Code.gs with the entire contents of `create-job-search-form.gs`.
3. Select `createJobSearchSurvey`, click Run, and review the Google authorization request. The script needs Forms and Sheets access to create the survey and its response destination.
4. Open the edit URL in the execution log. The form is created as an unpublished draft. The linked response sheet stays private.
5. Review the wording and settings. Publish with responder access for anyone with the link. Workspace account policies can restrict external access; verify this in a signed-out browser.
6. Test consent decline; India → state → search; another listed country → correct region; a country without a region dropdown → free text; location privacy skip; no recent activity → outlook; recent activity → applications. Do not mix test responses into the analysis.
7. Replace [FORM LINK] in the Reddit draft with the responder URL. Check each community’s rules before posting.

The builder is not executed here: it has been checked locally for syntax and routing using an in-memory simulation, not against your Google account. Running it again in the same Apps Script project returns the existing form without duplicating questions. If an earlier run failed, the log reports “building”; inspect the partial form and resolve the cause before starting again in a fresh project. A fresh project creates a separate form.

## Country branching limitation

Google Forms branches by section from multiple-choice or dropdown answers; it does not provide a native cascading country/state field. Google documents a 75-section limit. A separate section for every country would exceed that limit, so this version supplies seven country-specific dropdowns and a free-text state/province/region fallback for every other country. It includes a broad country/territory list and an “Other / not listed” escape hatch. UK detail is the constituent nation, not county. Additional priority countries can replace or extend the configured lists within the section limit.

## Preparing for a public dashboard

- Keep the response Sheet private. Build a separate aggregation step that exposes only summary statistics to the website; never embed or publish the raw response Sheet.
- Retain the automatic submission timestamp and add a survey version / round to your analysis. Keep wording stable within a round. A submission date describes when a report was made, not when every application occurred.
- The Data dictionary tab maps stable field IDs to Google Form item IDs. Collapse the country-specific region columns into one normalized region field when processing responses. The country field’s values are held in the script’s SURVEY.countries list.
- Exclude consent declines and clearly invalid/test submissions. Distinguish active seekers, recent successful seekers and passive respondents. Exclude “not applicable,” “not sure” and missing answers from each applicable denominator and show their counts separately.
- Display the number of eligible respondents alongside every percentage. These are respondent-reported bands, not exact application-level response or rejection rates. Do not treat “about half” as an observed 50% conversion rate or average ordinal labels into a precise rate.
- As an initial privacy rule, suppress breakdowns with fewer than 10 respondents, including combinations that allow a small group to be inferred by subtraction. Review sparse cross-filters; 10 alone is not a guarantee of anonymity. Do not expose individual rows or unreviewed written feedback.
- Label results “Experiences reported by survey respondents,” rather than population unemployment statistics or a representative measure of the whole job market. Reddit respondents are self-selected. Do not infer that a platform or country causes an outcome from these comparisons.
- Multi-select channels overlap. This survey cannot attribute a response or offer to a specific platform. It also does not measure exact offer conversion, daily calendar-average application counts, or confirm whether a listing is genuine.
- Turn off email collection and automatic public response summaries. Keeping sign-in and one-response-per-account restrictions off improves access but allows duplicate submissions; this survey cannot promise verified uniqueness. Do not claim it has verified identities.
- Choose a collection cadence before treating this as a trend tracker. For example, run separate monthly rounds with one response per person per round and label overlapping rolling windows clearly. This initial form is one round, not a configured recurring study.
- Use a later, separate survey for named company reviews. This first survey deliberately requests no company names, so it can describe broad experiences but cannot rank or verify companies. A review platform needs a published moderation, evidence, dispute and recency policy before publishing accusations or scores.

## Practical pilot

Ask 3–5 people from different situations to try the unpublished preview and time themselves before advertising a completion time. The 5–7 minute estimate is provisional. The longest route includes optional questions; respondents without recent hiring activity skip the applications section. Review the privacy wording and who can access the response Sheet before collecting real responses.
