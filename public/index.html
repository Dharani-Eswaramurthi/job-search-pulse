How this was run

I am Dharani Eswaramurthi. Job Search Pulse is a personal project, not a company, recruiter, or employer. The form and the code are mine. The source is on GitHub.

What you are looking at

Answers go to a Google Form and a private Google Sheet. A small server program reads only the timestamp and the fixed-choice questions. Names, emails, detailed location, and written comments are not fetched and are not shown here. The download is the same tables you see on the page, not a list of people.

This is not a population statistic

People choose whether to take part. Recruitment through Reddit and other communities does not represent every job seeker. These figures cannot stand in for national unemployment, and they cannot explain why someone got an offer. Identities are not checked. Anonymous forms can be filled more than once. Totals are not verified unique people.

What the questions cover

Most questions ask about the past 30 days. Questions about employer replies refer to applications sent 14–30 days before the person answered, so there was some time to hear back. An “application received” note is not counted as a decision or a next step.

What “communication stopped” means here

An employer had invited the person to continue, or had spoken with them, then missed an agreed update date by at least 7 days — or gave no update for at least 14 days when no date was agreed. A clear rejection or an explained delay does not count.

How the percentages are built

Each chart uses its own group of eligible answers. Percentages are of people, not of applications. Missing, “not sure,” and not-applicable answers are left out of that chart. Some questions allow more than one answer, so those bars can add up to more than 100%. Hiring stages show the furthest stage a person reported, not a conversion funnel.

Small groups

Small groups and small answer categories are withheld. An extra category may be withheld so a small count cannot be worked out from the total.

Withholding reduces the chance that someone can be picked out of a table. It is not a formal anonymity guarantee. Overlapping groups and changing totals can still allow guesses.

How fresh this page is

While the tab is open, it asks for a new summary on a short interval. Updates usually show up within a few minutes because results are cached. The weekly chart counts when people submitted the form; it does not measure whether the job market got better or worse. Please fill the form once per survey round.
